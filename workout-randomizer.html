Training Roll

Resistance training. Each roll gives one exercise per training type, matched so all five movement groups (Push, Pull, Core, Legs, Full Body) are covered exactly once. Lock cards to keep them, re-roll the rest. Tap the stats to edit records, or Log once you've done it.

Every exercise in your program - resistance, focus, cardio and mobility. Tap any to view or edit its RM and record. A hollow ○ marks exercises with no numbers yet.

Rolls one cardio exercise at random. Bag & Rope Work is a single combined session covering Punching Bag, Speed Bag, Line Jumps, and Ropes. Tap Swap to choose a specific exercise instead.

Tap a muscle on the diagram to get one randomly-picked exercise for it, weighted the same way as the main roll — recently-logged picks are less likely to come up again. Lock it to keep it, Swap to choose a specific one of the three instead.

One random pick each for Forearm and Band work — independent of the main roll. Lock any card to keep it while you re-roll the others.

Rolls one of four mobility sessions at random: Yoga Flow, PNF Stretches, Pilates, or Foam, Balance & Rope. Each bundles its full routine into one exercise. Tap Swap to choose a specific one instead.

Records and logs persist in this browser. RM = your listed rep max · type record = working prescription for that modality.

Send to Apple Health

Apple gives web pages no access to Health data, so this hands the workout to the Shortcuts app, which logs it for you. One-time setup below, then it's a single tap after each session.

One-time setup in the Shortcuts app:

  1. New shortcut, named exactly what's in the box above.
  2. Add Get Dictionary from Input.
  3. Add Log Workout. Set Type to Traditional Strength Training.
  4. For Duration, pick the minutes key from the dictionary.
  5. Optional: add Show Result so you can see it worked.
  6. Enable Shortcuts › Advanced › Allow Running Scripts.

The payload also carries date, exercises, detail and volume if you want to log a note or extra samples.
